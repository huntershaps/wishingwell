Before anyone buys one

You need your ring size, and it is the one thing a gifter cannot guess. Most of these list sizes 5 to 13. Measure the finger you would actually wear it on, late in the day when your hands are at their largest, and put the number on the wishlist item so nobody has to ask and spoil it.

Sterling tarnishes and stainless and tungsten do not. If you would rather never think about it, the tarnish-resistant and rhodium-plated ones above are the answer. Tungsten cannot be resized at all, which makes it the riskiest thing here to receive as a gift.
